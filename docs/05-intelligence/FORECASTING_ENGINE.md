# Forecasting Engine

## Forecast domains

Income, expenses, balances, asset values, liabilities, taxes, business performance, and goal funding.

## Method

Prefer simple transparent models when adequate; introduce complexity only when validation shows material benefit.

## Requirements

Ranges, confidence bands, seasonality where relevant, scenario overrides, drift monitoring, and backtesting.

## Calibration

Compare forecast versus actual, record error by horizon and domain, and adjust models without altering historical forecasts.

## Data quality

Incomplete or stale inputs widen uncertainty and may block high-impact recommendations.

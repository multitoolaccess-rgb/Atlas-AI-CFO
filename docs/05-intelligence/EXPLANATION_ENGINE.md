# Explanation Engine

## Purpose

Turn analysis into faithful, understandable explanations without changing the underlying conclusion.

## Required questions

What changed? Why does it matter? What is recommended? What is the impact? What are the risks and alternatives? What data and assumptions were used? What could change the conclusion?

## Levels

Executive summary, standard explanation, and technical detail share the same evidence and decision record.

## Rules

No fabricated causal claims, unsupported precision, hidden caveats, or persuasive language that overstates confidence.

## Accessibility

Use plain language, clear units, readable charts, and definitions for financial terms.

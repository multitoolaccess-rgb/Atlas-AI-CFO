# Goal Engine

## Purpose

Maintain goal state, probability, dependencies, and required trajectory.

## Calculations

Use deterministic models for cash flow, compounding, taxes, inflation, and withdrawals. Use scenarios or Monte Carlo where uncertainty matters.

## Outputs

Current probability range, target gap, most influential variables, required actions, sensitivities, and conflicts among goals.

## Updates

Recalculate on material data changes, assumption changes, accepted decisions, and scheduled reviews. Preserve prior projections for comparison.

## Explainability

Expose model version, assumptions, data freshness, and drivers of probability change.

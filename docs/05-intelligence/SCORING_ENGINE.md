# Scoring Engine

## Purpose

Provide consistent ranking across risks, recommendations, opportunities, and goals.

## Dimensions

Impact, probability, confidence, urgency, goal alignment, downside, effort, reversibility, liquidity, and data quality.

## Principles

Scores support judgment; they do not conceal evidence. Normalize by object type, show component contributions, and avoid false precision.

## Governance

Version formulas, test edge cases, monitor distribution drift, and require review before changing production weights.

## User impact

Personal preferences may change ranking within safe bounds but cannot suppress critical risks or policy violations.

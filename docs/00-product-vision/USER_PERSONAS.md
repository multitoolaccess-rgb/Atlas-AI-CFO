# Atlas User Personas

## Wealth Owner

Wants to know whether the household is on track, which actions matter, what risks are emerging, and how to create freedom sooner. Needs direct explanations, simulations, and control.

## Household Collaborator

A spouse or family member needs a shared but appropriately scoped view of goals, trade-offs, obligations, and decisions.

## Financial Advisor

Needs cross-client monitoring, preparation assistance, explainable findings, collaboration, and evidence—not opaque automated advice.

## Future Family-Office Operator

Needs entity ownership, complex assets, permissions, workflows, reporting, and governance.

## Persona rule

Atlas adapts depth and presentation by role while maintaining one canonical financial truth and strict least-privilege access.

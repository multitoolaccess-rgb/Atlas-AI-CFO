# Atlas Product Principles

1. Freedom is the outcome; wealth is an instrument.
2. Goals drive prioritization.
3. Trust is the product.
4. Financial truth is deterministic, traceable, and timestamped.
5. Intelligence is proactive but never coercive.
6. Recommendations expose evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and alternatives.
7. Human control precedes autonomy.
8. Household collaboration respects privacy and ownership.
9. High-impact simplicity beats dashboard density.
10. Atlas learns from decisions and outcomes without rewriting history.

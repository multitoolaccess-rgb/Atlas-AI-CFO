# Atlas AI CFO — Product Requirements Document

**Version:** 1.0

## Purpose

Atlas is an AI Chief Financial Officer that continuously evaluates the user’s financial position, predicts outcomes, identifies opportunities, and recommends actions. Its objective is to optimize life outcomes.

## Product goals

Atlas helps users build and protect wealth, retire earlier, reduce taxes and risk, discover opportunities, make better decisions, and feel confident that they remain on track.

## Primary users

- **Phase 1:** Individuals and families pursuing financial independence, including professionals, business owners, and investors.
- **Phase 2:** Financial advisors serving multiple clients.

## Core journey

1. **Learn:** Collect income, expenses, assets, investments, debts, insurance, businesses, and goals.
2. **Understand:** Calculate net worth, cash flow, savings rate, goal probability, risks, and opportunity scores.
3. **Predict:** Project wealth, retirement, cash flow, investment growth, taxes, and business outcomes.
4. **Recommend:** Suggest specific, ranked actions.
5. **Execute:** Begin with explicit approval; evolve toward policy-governed autonomy.

## Product modules

### Wealth Command Center

Shows current position, future projection, goal progress, portfolio, cash flow, recommendations, and alerts.

### Goal Engine

Maintains financial and life goals. Every recommendation maps to at least one goal.

### Recommendation Engine

Ranks actions by impact, confidence, risk, time horizon, preferences, and goal alignment. Each recommendation includes rationale, benefits, risks, trade-offs, and confidence.

### Opportunity Engine

Searches for investment, business, tax, income, and real-estate opportunities.

### Simulation Lab

Models retirement dates, business launches, real-estate purchases, investment changes, spending changes, and relocation.

### Decision Journal

Stores the decision, rationale, expected impact, action taken, actual outcome, and lessons learned.

## Explainability requirements

Every recommendation answers:

- What changed?
- Why does it matter?
- What action is recommended?
- What happens if ignored?
- What data and assumptions were used?
- How confident is Atlas?

## Non-goals

Atlas does not aim to become tax-filing, accounting, banking, or brokerage software. It integrates with those systems.

## Success metrics

Primary: probability of achieving user goals.

Secondary: net-worth growth, tax savings, investment efficiency, opportunities discovered, recommendation acceptance, measured decision quality, and user trust.

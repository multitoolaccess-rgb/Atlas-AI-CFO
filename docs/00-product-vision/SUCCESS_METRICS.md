# Atlas Success Metrics

## North-star outcome

Change in probability of achieving the user’s prioritized financial goals, measured with transparent assumptions.

## Outcome metrics

Goal probability, projected retirement age, tax savings, avoidable fees, risk reduction, liquidity resilience, and opportunity value realized.

## Decision metrics

Recommendation usefulness, acceptance or reasoned rejection, time to decision, forecast calibration, and outcome measurement coverage.

## Trust metrics

Data accuracy, correction rate, explanation usefulness, permission incidents, user-reported confidence, and advisor review agreement.

## Guardrails

Never optimize engagement, recommendation volume, or acceptance rate at the expense of suitability, accuracy, or autonomy.

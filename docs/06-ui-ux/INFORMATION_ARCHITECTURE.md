# Information Architecture

## Organizing principle

Organize around user intent and decisions, not financial institution or data type.

## Top level

- Mission Control: what matters now
- Goals: destination and trajectory
- Decisions: questions, options, and journal
- Simulations: future scenarios
- Investments: portfolio intelligence
- Opportunities: ranked growth paths
- Advisor: controlled collaboration
- Data & Settings: sources, profile, permissions, policies, and audit

## Cross-links

Every insight links to affected goals, supporting data, relevant scenarios, and decision history.

## State

Clearly distinguish draft, current, stale, at risk, pending approval, executed, and measured.

# Delivery Milestones

## M1: Verified baseline

Accurate net worth, cash flow, data freshness, and goal setup.

## M2: Explainable trajectory

Versioned forecasts, probability ranges, and driver explanations.

## M3: Trusted recommendations

Ranked actions with evidence, alternatives, confidence, and evaluation.

## M4: Decision system

Simulation, decision journal, and outcome measurement.

## M5: Household and advisor collaboration

Role-based sharing, review, comments, and audit.

## M6: Execution readiness

Policies, approvals, previews, reconciliation, incident controls, and independent readiness review.

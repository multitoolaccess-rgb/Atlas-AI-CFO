# Atlas 12-Month Roadmap

## Quarter 1: Foundation

Canonical household model, data-quality layer, goals, baseline forecasts, audit events, and Mission Control skeleton.

## Quarter 2: Intelligence

Recommendation contract, goal engine, decision journal, risk detection, explanation experience, and evaluation harness.

## Quarter 3: Decisions

Simulation Lab, investment intelligence, opportunity discovery, household collaboration, and outcome tracking.

## Quarter 4: Advisor and readiness

Advisor sharing, review workflows, advanced permissions, production hardening, and limited prepared-action pilots.

## Gate

No autonomous money movement in year one without separate security, legal, operational, and measured-reliability approval.

# Atlas Goal Model

**Version:** 1.0

## Purpose

Goals are the foundation of Atlas intelligence. Every recommendation must connect to at least one user goal.

## Philosophy

Money is a means to life outcomes such as retirement freedom, family security, business ownership, education, lifestyle, and legacy.

## Goal structure

- Objective and category
- Quantified target
- Target date or age
- Priority
- Owner and beneficiaries
- Constraints and non-negotiables
- Funding sources
- Assumptions
- Current status and probability

## Examples

- Retire at 55.
- Reach $15M net worth by age 60.
- Build $250,000 in annual passive income.
- Start or acquire a business.
- Fund children’s education.

## Probability

Atlas calculates a range and probability using current state, contributions, return and inflation assumptions, taxes, cash-flow needs, and uncertainty. It exposes assumptions and sensitivity.

## Dependencies and conflicts

Goals may share resources or conflict. Atlas models trade-offs, sequencing, priority, and household impact rather than optimizing goals independently.

## Lifecycle

Draft → Active → At risk → Adjusted → Achieved, abandoned, or superseded.

Every change is historical and attributable.

## Core rule

No recommendation exists without goal context, except urgent actions required to protect security, solvency, or legal compliance.

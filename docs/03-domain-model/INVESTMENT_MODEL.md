# Atlas Investment Model

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Foundational domain model

## Purpose

Define how Atlas understands investments and supports long-term, goal-aligned decisions. Atlas is an investment intelligence system, not a short-term trading product.

## Philosophy

Optimize goal achievement, risk-adjusted returns, tax efficiency, liquidity, and long-term wealth—not market timing or maximum return without regard to risk.

## Model

An investment links a security, account, owner, quantity, cost basis, valuation, performance, risk, tax characteristics, liquidity, and goal alignment.

## Portfolio dimensions

- Asset-class allocation
- Sector and geographic exposure
- Single-security and employer concentration
- Account location and tax treatment
- Fees and turnover
- Liquidity and time horizon
- Expected return, volatility, and drawdown

## Health assessment

Diversification, cost efficiency, tax efficiency, goal alignment, risk fit, liquidity, and data quality.

## Recommendation example

Reduce a concentrated holding because it exceeds the user’s policy target. Quantify the proposed change, estimated risk reduction, tax consequences, alternatives, and confidence.

## Autonomy progression

Analyze → Recommend → User-approved order → Policy-governed execution.

Policies may specify allocation bands, rebalancing cadence, tax constraints, prohibited assets, and maximum trade sizes.

## Investment memory

Store philosophy, constraints, recommendations, user choices, executions, and outcomes.

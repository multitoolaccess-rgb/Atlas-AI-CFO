# Atlas User Profile Model

**Version:** 1.0

## Purpose

Understand the person and household behind the financial data. Identical balance sheets can require different advice because values, goals, constraints, and lives differ.

## Profile dimensions

- Identity, age, location, career, and employment
- Household members, dependents, and responsibilities
- Financial philosophy and values
- Goals and life vision
- Risk capacity, tolerance, and required risk
- Liquidity and time-horizon needs
- Skills, interests, experience, and network
- Lifestyle and retirement preferences
- Communication and decision style
- Automation preference and consent

## Risk model

Distinguish investment tolerance from financial capacity, income dependency, business appetite, liquidity needs, and behavioral response to loss.

## Opportunity context

Skills, experience, available time, capital, network, and interests inform business, career, and income opportunities.

## Personalization

Recommendations combine validated financial data, user profile, goals, household context, constraints, and preferences. Atlas never recommends from balances alone.

## Governance

Profile facts identify source and confidence. Sensitive inferences are visible, editable, and never treated as confirmed without appropriate user validation.

# Atlas Opportunity Model

**Version:** 1.0

## Purpose

Define how Atlas discovers, evaluates, ranks, and tracks opportunities that may improve the user’s financial future.

## Philosophy

Traditional applications analyze existing money. Atlas also searches for responsible ways to create wealth.

## Categories

- Investments and allocation improvements
- Businesses, acquisitions, franchises, and consulting
- Career advancement and skill monetization
- Tax strategies
- Real estate
- Financing, insurance, and cost optimization

## Opportunity record

- Category and description
- Source and discovery date
- User fit and linked goals
- Required capital, time, skills, and dependencies
- Expected return or savings as a range
- Downside, liquidity, and concentration risk
- Probability and confidence
- Tax, legal, and operational considerations
- Recommended next diligence step
- Expiration or review date

## Scoring

Rank by expected value, probability, goal alignment, required effort, downside, reversibility, liquidity, time to impact, user fit, and data quality.

## Example

A consulting business may require modest capital and ten hours per week, produce a range of potential income, and accelerate retirement. Atlas should recommend exploration or diligence—not present uncertain forecasts as guarantees.

## Discovery sources

User skills and preferences, available capital, financial data, approved external data, market context, and advisor input.

## Memory

Track recommended, explored, accepted, rejected, expired, and completed opportunities plus their actual outcomes.

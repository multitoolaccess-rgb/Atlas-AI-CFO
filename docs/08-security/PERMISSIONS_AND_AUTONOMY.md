# Permissions and Autonomy

## Permission dimensions

Actor, resource, action, amount, destination, time window, frequency, and approval requirement.

## Autonomy levels

Read, analyze, recommend, prepare, execute with confirmation, and execute within policy.

## Rules

Permissions are explicit, narrow, revocable, and visible. High-risk actions require stronger authentication and may require human review regardless of policy.

## Policy examples

Maximum trade value, allowed accounts and assets, allocation bands, prohibited transfers, daily limits, and expiration date.

## Safety

Stale data, conflicting instructions, unusual destinations, policy ambiguity, or model uncertainty blocks execution.

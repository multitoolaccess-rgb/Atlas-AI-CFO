# Competitive Analysis

## Alternatives

- Aggregators and budgeting apps: strong history, weak forward action.
- Planning tools: strong scenarios, often episodic and advisor-centered.
- Robo-advisors: strong portfolio automation, narrow life context.
- General AI assistants: flexible reasoning, weak canonical data and controls.
- Human advisors: judgment and trust, limited continuous monitoring.

## Atlas advantage

Continuous goal-aware intelligence, decision history, multi-domain context, explainable recommendations, and staged autonomy.

## Risks

Incumbent distribution, data-integration fragility, regulatory boundaries, trust failures, and generic AI commoditization.

## Response

Focus on private personalized memory, financial truth, outcome calibration, safe workflows, and advisor complementarity.

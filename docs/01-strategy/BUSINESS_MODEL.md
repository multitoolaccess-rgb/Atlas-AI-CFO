# Atlas Business Model Strategy

**Version:** 1.0

## Vision

Atlas serves individuals, families, advisors, wealth-management firms, and eventually family offices.

## Phase 1: Consumer subscriptions

### Atlas Personal

Financial overview, AI coaching, goal tracking, simulations, and recommendations.

### Atlas Wealth

Investment intelligence, tax optimization, business analysis, household collaboration, and advanced simulations.

## Phase 2: Advisor SaaS

Advisors pay for client intelligence, automated reviews, portfolio analysis, recommendation workflows, and client communication.

## Phase 3: Enterprise

Serve wealth-management firms, family offices, and selected financial institutions with governance, integrations, and administration.

## Pricing philosophy

Compete on improved outcomes, not low price. Pricing should reflect value while avoiding incentives that compromise advice.

## Business principles

1. Align revenue with user success.
2. Never sell personal financial data.
3. Preserve trust over short-term revenue.
4. Disclose conflicts and referral economics.

## Partnerships

Potential partners include banks, brokerages, tax and payroll platforms, insurers, and real-estate systems.

## Long-term opportunity

Become the intelligence layer connecting money, decisions, opportunities, and life goals.

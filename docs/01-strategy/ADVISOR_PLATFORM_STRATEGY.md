# Atlas Advisor Platform Strategy

**Version:** 1.0

## Vision

Atlas should not replace great advisors; it should make them substantially more capable.

## Opportunity

Advisors spend significant time gathering data, preparing reports, reviewing portfolios, creating recommendations, and explaining concepts. Atlas automates intelligence work while preserving human judgment and relationships.

## Value proposition

The AI intelligence platform for modern financial advisors.

## Capabilities

- Client financial health, goal progress, risks, and opportunities
- Automated quarterly reviews, portfolio summaries, and goal updates
- Identification of drift, tax opportunities, insurance gaps, and retirement risks
- Simulations and recommendation drafting

## Human role

The advisor remains accountable for advice and the trusted relationship. Atlas provides analysis, predictions, alternatives, and explanations.

## Sharing and permissions

Clients control access. Roles may include read-only, planning, investment analysis, and full advisory collaboration. Access is revocable and auditable.

## Target workflow

Client data → Atlas intelligence → Advisor review → Client decision → Outcome tracking.

## Long-term vision

Atlas becomes intelligence infrastructure for the next generation of financial advisors.

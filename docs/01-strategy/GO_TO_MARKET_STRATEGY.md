# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Beachhead

Founder-led reference implementation for a complex, high-income household targeting financial independence, business ownership, and $5M–$20M net worth.

## Sequence

1. Deliver trusted read-only intelligence for the reference household.
2. Invite a small cohort with similar complexity.
3. Validate advisor collaboration with user-controlled sharing.
4. Launch advisor SaaS after consumer intelligence proves reliable.

## Acquisition

Educational financial-decision content, advisor referrals, founder networks, and high-intent planning use cases.

## Activation

The user receives a verified financial baseline, goal probability, top risks, and three explainable priority actions.

## Expansion

Household collaboration, advanced simulation, advisor review, and optional execution workflows.
